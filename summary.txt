News, Wednesday 11 November 2026.

Russia and Ukraine ended two days of US-mediated Abu Dhabi talks, first in nine months. Reuters reported a prisoner exchange agreement but no ceasefire, with the Donbas deadlocked. Jared Kushner said there was “no trust”; he and Steve Witkoff were to meet Vladimir Putin in Moscow Thursday, then Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Ireland. Reuters reported Russian strikes on six electricity substations in central Ukraine and a market attack in the Odesa region that killed three.

Iran designated Argentina’s navy and air force terrorist organisations, Reuters reported. Security chief Mohsen Rezaei warned of attacks on distant US bases if the US struck again. UK Maritime Trade Operations confirmed a projectile hit a tanker off Oman Tuesday; Iran’s Revolutionary Guards claimed three tankers hit mines, a claim Reuters could not verify. The New York Times, citing sources, reported Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent moved to a military base amid Iranian threats.

Israeli strikes killed three Palestinians in Gaza, including two brothers in a tent for displaced people, medics told Reuters; Israel said it struck militants.

NATO scrambled jets after at least four drones strayed into Baltic airspace during Ukrainian strikes on Russia, Reuters reported, prompting shelter alerts in Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia. No one was injured.

At COP31 in Antalya, leaders began two days of speeches. Reuters reported Antonio Guterres warned the climate crisis was “outrunning us” on a “path to mutually assured destruction.” Turkey sought 35% electricity in energy use by 2035; the US skipped the talks for a second year.

British police arrested a 41-year-old US Army soldier near Gloucester on Tuesday on suspicion of spying for Russia, the BBC reported. He served with NATO’s Allied Rapid Reaction Corps and was uncharged.

A CloudSek report said a Kazakhstan-linked group used an AI agent framework to steal over 2,000 national-security documents from Tajikistan (Reuters).
