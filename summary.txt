Wednesday, November 11, 2026

Reuters said US-facilitated Abu Dhabi talks ended after two days with a prisoner swap but no ceasefire; Donbas remained deadlocked. These were the first Russian-Ukrainian sessions in nine months. Jared Kushner said “no trust.” He and Steve Witkoff were due Thursday in Moscow with Vladimir Putin, then Ireland with Volodymyr Zelenskyy. Reuters also reported strikes on six Ukrainian power substations and three deaths in an Odesa marketplace attack.

Reuters reported Iran listed Argentina’s naval and air branches as terrorist organisations. Mohsen Rezaei warned Iran would hit US bases if attacked again. The UK Maritime Trade Operations office confirmed a projectile hit an oil tanker near Oman Tuesday. The Revolutionary Guards’ claim that three tankers hit mines remains unverified; Reuters could not confirm it. The New York Times, citing unnamed sources, reported Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent moved to a military base over Iranian threats.

Medical workers told Reuters Israeli strikes killed three Palestinians in Gaza, including two brothers in a tent for displaced people. Israel said it targeted militants.

Reuters said NATO scrambled fighters after at least four drones entered Baltic airspace during Ukrainian strikes on Russia. Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia urged shelter; nobody hurt.

COP31 opened in Antalya; leaders were due to speak over two days. Reuters said Antonio Guterres warned the climate crisis was “outrunning us” and the world on a “path to mutually assured destruction.” Turkey aims for electricity to supply 35% of energy use by 2035. The United States stayed away for a second year.

The BBC reported British police detained a 41-year-old US Army soldier near Gloucester Tuesday on suspicion of spying for Russia. He served in NATO’s Allied Rapid Reaction Corps; uncharged.

Reuters reported CloudSek described a Kazakhstan-connected group using AI agents to steal over 2,000 documents on Tajikistan national security.
